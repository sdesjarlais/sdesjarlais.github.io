103Beddigton

A private, single-user tool that lets its owner manage their own Google mail and calendar from their own computer. It is not offered to the public.

Privacy policy

103Beddigton is used only by the person who created it, on their own account.

  • It accesses Google data (Gmail and Google Calendar) only after the account owner signs in and grants permission.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored locally in the computer's encrypted keyring and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Terms

This is a personal tool with no public users. It is provided as-is.
